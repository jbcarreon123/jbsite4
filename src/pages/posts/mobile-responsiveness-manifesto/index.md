# Note
If you get offended, this manifesto doesn't mean to attack anyone and only shows my opinions about the indie web today and the different attitudes of different people about the topic of mobile responsiveness.
If you're disagreeing with my opinions, you can respond to it by writing an article yourselves! It would be fun to respond back to disagreements and such. Do not go to my guestbook and complain there.

# You're using a phone,
## and the site you're visiting doesn't work on phones.
And it shouldn't have to be. The indie web is for everybody, but I feel like mobile users are kept separate and treated differently.

Responsiveness doesn't also mean you're just making your site to be responsive so mobile users can use your site, it also means that other people that don't have the same browser viewport as you can view your site fine.

## and you are being mentioned to be "a chump".
It's not really nice to treat mobile users differently, they're humans after all. And also, mobile contributes to over 60% of users on the Internet, and it's not really good if you treat the majority of users and shun them.

Most manifestos there also states that corporate web is not user friendly, and I am going to also state that your mobile hostile site is also not user friendly.

# The worse part is,
## those sites are already capable of being mobile responsive.
I know, responsive design is not magic where you implement it with a simple click and your site gets mobile friendly automagically. It requires some code to make your site responsive, and it can be worth it for everyone.

## they are already using modern web principles.
Things like flexboxes, and grids, are mostly used by indie web sites to layout their pages, and it would be great if it will also make webmasters consider mobile responsiveness, but it's not.

# You're making a new site,
## and how can I make my site responsive?
You can utilize flexboxes and grids in your layout! It can also serve as the framework of making your site responsive, so use it!

You can also use things like media queries, and stuff so your layout can easily adapt to your needs in mobile mode.
